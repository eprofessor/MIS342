Assignment Overview — LuckyTire

LuckyTire originally collected sales information in one flat file. Customer and tire information is repeated every time a sale occurs. You will compare that flat structure with a normalized three-table version and then build the relationships in Access.

Formative02 is the one assignment in this sequence where you build the normalized foundation yourself. Beginning with Formative03, each Formative supplies a clean instructor-normalized LuckyTire starter database so earlier mistakes do not interfere with the new skill.

This is a formative assignment. The goal is to learn by building, testing, and checking the database work. You do not need to submit screenshots, a PDF, or Digital Artifact Notebook evidence.

Exercise 1 — Compare, Build, and Verify a Normalized LuckyTire Structure
  1. Download and open LuckyTire_FlatSales.csv. Identify at least two pieces of customer or tire information that repeat across multiple sales rows.
  2. Create a blank Access database named Formative02_LuckyTire.accdb.
  3. Import LuckyTire_tblCustomer.csv as tblCustomer. Set CustID as the primary key and verify the data types.
  4. Import LuckyTire_tblTires.csv as tblTires. Set PartID as the primary key. Verify that UnitPrice is stored as an appropriate numeric/currency type.
  5. Import LuckyTire_tblSales.csv as tblSales. Set Invoice as the primary key. Verify that CustID and PartID use compatible data types with their related primary keys.
  6. Open Database Tools > Relationships. Create a one-to-many relationship from tblCustomer.CustID to tblSales.CustID and enforce referential integrity.
  7. Create a one-to-many relationship from tblTires.PartID to tblSales.PartID and enforce referential integrity.
  8. Verify that the Relationships window shows 1 on the customer/tire sides and ∞ on the sales side.
  9. Open all three tables and compare them with LuckyTire_FlatSales.csv. Confirm that a customer address and a tire price are stored once in the normalized structure rather than repeated in every sale.
  10. Save and close Formative02_LuckyTire.accdb.
