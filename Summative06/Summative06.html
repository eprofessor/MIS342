Assignment Purpose and Learning Outcomes

Formative06 compared form types and usability. Summative06 requires you to create several LuckyTire form interfaces, match each to a business task, and test both data behavior and usability.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Create a Multiple Items form for scanning multiple records.
  2. Create and customize a Split Form.
  3. Build a main form/subform using related data.
  4. Add and verify a calculated control.
  5. Develop and execute form test cases for usability and correct data behavior.

Primary course learning-outcome alignment: CLO 5, with supporting evidence for CLOs 8 and 11.

Questions 1 through 6 require concise answers of no more than 500 characters. The Final Assignment Reflection allows a longer response.

Exercise 3 — Customer/Sales Form-Subform
  1. Create or refine a customer main form that displays related sales in a subform.
